📋 Namma Space Hybrid AI Architecture & SOP

Step 1 • Frontend Guided Recorder

360° Direction Ring & Telemetry

src/liveScanner.js coaches rotation speed, filters blur, and attaches {yaw_deg, pitch_deg, pos_x, pos_z, is_doorway} to each sharp keyframe.

Step 2 • Python AI Backend

YOLOv8n + Floor-Horizon Depth

pipeline/server.py runs pre-trained YOLOv8n + OpenCV structural segmentation to detect furniture (`sofa`, `chair`, `table`, `bed`, `cabinet`) and wall depth.

Step 3 • Multi-Room Stitching

Auto-Doorway & Clean 3D Meshes

src/semantic3DBuilder.js stitches multiple rooms with walkable doorways and renders clean 3D furniture models instead of noisy point clouds.

Step 4 • A* & Live AR Navigation

Obstacle-Aware Routing

Real-time 8-way A* pathfinding around walls and AI-detected furniture, plus live smartphone rear-camera AR arrow overlays.
